🛡️ Our Zero Audio Retention Promise

SyncDub AI never records, archives, stores, or sells your audio. Whether capturing video playback audio via Android MediaProjection or loopback microphone audio, incoming sound chunks are converted in volatile memory (RAM) exclusively to generate real-time live translated speech and are discarded instantaneously. Your conversations and videos remain 100% private to you.

02 Information We Collect & How We Use It

A. Real-Time Audio Data (Ephemeral Processing Only)

To perform live dubbing of playing videos or spoken speech, SyncDub AI captures audio streams using:

  • Microphone Input (RECORD_AUDIO): Captures spoken speech or speaker playback when physical microphone mode is active.
  • Internal Playback Audio Capture (Android 10+ MediaProjection): With your explicit one-time system confirmation, captures the internal digital audio of media playing on your phone (e.g., YouTube, social video apps) directly without room noise.

Processing Pipeline: Captured 16kHz PCM audio buffers are streamed via encrypted TLS 1.3 WebSockets directly to the neural speech synthesis engine. Once translation is streamed back, the audio chunk is immediately purged from RAM. Audio is never written to disk, stored on servers, or used for model re-training.

B. Account & Authentication Data

When you authenticate using Google Sign-In, we receive standard OAuth 2.0 public identity tokens managed securely through Supabase Auth:

  • Your Name and Email Address (for identification and account recovery).
  • Profile picture URL (optional display on your in-app profile card).
  • Unique Supabase user identifier (UUID).

We do not access your Google passwords, contacts, Google Drive files, or any unrelated account permissions.

C. Subscription & Quota Usage

To manage your free trial minutes (e.g., 45 minutes Starter) or paid Pro/Studio quotas (e.g., 3,000 to 10,000 minutes), we store your active plan tier and remaining translation minutes balance securely in your Supabase user profile.

D. Local Device Preferences

SyncDub AI stores user settings locally on your device via SharedPreferences:

  • Target translation language selection (e.g., Hindi, Spanish, French, Japanese).
  • Preferred audio capture mode (Internal Video Capture vs. Microphone Loopback).
  • Floating dubbing icon preferences (docking position, overlay size, dubbed subtitles toggle).
  • UI theme mode (Light Mode / Dark Mode).
